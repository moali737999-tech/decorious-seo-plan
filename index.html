Starting point

What works for us, and what works against us

Working for us

  • No signs of a Google penalty.
  • The site's existing links still work, and Google has started bringing them back.
  • A large library of articles in Arabic and English.

Working against us

  • Service pages are short compared with competitors.
  • Several pages compete for the same search.
  • The homepage is slow on mobile.
What we will do

Action plan

  • We will identify the pages that brought in the most visits before the shutdown, make sure they work as before, and republish important articles that were removed.
  • We will ask Google directly to recrawl the 30 most important pages, so they return faster.
  • We will update the Decorious profile on Google Maps (address, opening hours, photos, WhatsApp).
  • We will update the homepage title and description in Arabic and English.
  • We will publish a guide to interior design costs in Dubai, in Arabic and English.
  • We will publish an in-depth article on villa renovation in Dubai. People clearly search for it, and the site doesn't cover it today.
  • We will organise page titles and the links between pages so that each search has a single page that represents it.
  • We will write articles about your standout projects, showing the location, size, timeline and before-and-after photos.
  • We will work on getting Decorious listed in the "best interior design companies in Dubai" lists that rank at the top of Google.
